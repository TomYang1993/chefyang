steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large sheet pan with parchment."
  - "Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp oil and 1/2 tsp salt. Toss the broccoli with the rest of the oil and salt. Spread both on the pan in a single layer."
  - "Roast for 18 to 20 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) inside."
  - "Meanwhile, simmer the honey, soy sauce, garlic, vinegar and cornstarch in a small pan, stirring, for 2 minutes until it thickens."
  - "Brush half the glaze over the chicken and roast 5 more minutes until sticky. Drizzle with the rest of the glaze and sprinkle with sesame seeds."

Sheet pan dinners only work when nothing crowds. Give the chicken and broccoli room on the pan and they roast with crisp edges instead of steaming.

The glaze goes on at the end, not the start. Honey burns at high heat, so a short final roast gives you a sticky coat without black spots.
