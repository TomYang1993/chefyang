  - "Meanwhile, stir the garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, sesame oil and chili oil together."
  - "Shake off the liquid from the cucumbers. Toss with the dressing and top with sesame seeds. Serve right away."

Smashing instead of slicing gives the cucumbers rough, cracked edges that grab the dressing. It's a classic Chinese cold dish, often served alongside noodles or dumplings.

Black vinegar is the traditional choice and adds a deep, slightly malty tang. Rice vinegar works if that's what you have.
