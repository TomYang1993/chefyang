ingredients:
  - "1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless chicken breast or thighs"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 tsp chili powder"
  - "1 tsp ground cumin"
  - "1 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "1 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1 tsp salt, divided"
  - "1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice"
  - "1 lime, zested and juiced"
  - "1/3 cup chopped cilantro"
  - "1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed"
  - "1 1/2 cups frozen corn, thawed"
  - "1 cup salsa"
steps:
  - "Cook the rice with 3/4 tsp salt as the package directs. Fluff it and stir in the lime zest, lime juice and cilantro."
  - "Meanwhile, rub the chicken with the oil, spices and remaining salt."
  - "Cook the chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes per side, until it reaches 165°F (74°C). Rest for 5 minutes, then slice."
  - "Warm the black beans and corn together in the same skillet for 2 to 3 minutes."
  - "Divide the rice, chicken, beans and corn among 5 containers. Cool for no more than an hour, then lid and refrigerate. Pack salsa separately."
  - "To serve, microwave a bowl with the lid vented for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring halfway, until steaming hot. Add salsa and any fresh toppings."
notes:
  - "Keeps for 4 days in the fridge. Freeze any bowls you won't eat by day 4."
  - "Add fresh toppings after reheating: avocado, sour cream, shredded lettuce or cheese."

Burrito bowls are one of the best meal prep meals because every part reheats well. The rice stays soft, the chicken stays juicy, and the salsa goes on fresh.

Food safety matters more when food sits for days. Cool everything quickly, refrigerate within an hour, and eat it within 4 days.
