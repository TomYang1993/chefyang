steps:
  - "Break up any clumps in the cold rice with your fingers. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Pour in the eggs, stir gently until just set but still soft, then slide them onto a plate."
  - "Add the remaining oil, the green onion whites and the peas. Stir-fry for 30 seconds."
  - "Add the rice and spread it across the pan. Let it sit for 30 seconds, then toss. Repeat for 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is hot and some grains are lightly toasted."
  - "Add the soy sauce around the edge of the pan, then the eggs, sesame oil, white pepper and green onion greens. Toss for 30 seconds, breaking up the eggs, and serve."

Good fried rice comes down to two things: cold rice and high heat. Chilled rice has dried out a little, so the grains stay separate and toast instead of turning to mush.

Cooking the eggs first and adding them back at the end keeps them soft. White pepper is the classic seasoning and gives that familiar takeout flavor.
