These noodles come from the same idea as the famous garlic noodles of San Francisco: lots of butter, lots of garlic, a little salt and savoriness from soy and oyster sauce. Everything is already in most pantries.

The only skill here is patience with the garlic. Cooked gently, it turns sweet and nutty. Rushed over high heat, it turns bitter, so keep the flame low.
