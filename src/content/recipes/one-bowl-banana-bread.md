ingredients:
  - "3 very ripe bananas (about 1 1/2 cups mashed)"
  - "1/3 cup (75 g) melted butter"
  - "3/4 cup (150 g) brown sugar"
  - "1 large egg, beaten"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/4 tsp salt"
  - "1/2 tsp cinnamon"
  - "1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour"
  - "1/2 cup chopped walnuts or chocolate chips (optional)"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan and line it with a strip of parchment."
  - "Mash the bananas in a large bowl with a fork until mostly smooth. Whisk in the melted butter."
  - "Whisk in the brown sugar, egg and vanilla. Sprinkle the baking soda, salt and cinnamon over the top and whisk them in."
  - "Add the flour and stir with a spatula until no dry streaks remain. Fold in the nuts or chocolate if using."
  - "Pour into the pan and bake for 50 to 60 minutes, until a skewer in the center comes out clean. If the top browns too fast, cover loosely with foil after 40 minutes."
  - "Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then lift out and cool on a rack before slicing."

Banana bread is one of the most forgiving bakes there is, and this version needs nothing but a bowl, a fork and a whisk. Melted butter means no creaming and no mixer.

The riper the bananas, the better the loaf. Black, soft bananas bring more sugar and flavor, so you can use less added sugar.
