steps:
  - "Melt the butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling, until it foams and you see brown specks and smell toasted nuts, 5 to 7 minutes. Pour it into a large bowl, scraping in the brown bits, and cool for 10 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment."
  - "Whisk both sugars into the butter. Whisk in the egg, yolk and vanilla until smooth and glossy, about 1 minute."
  - "Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt until just combined, then fold in the chocolate."
  - "Scoop 2-tablespoon balls 2 inches apart. Bake one sheet at a time for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centers look slightly underdone."
  - "Sprinkle with flaky salt. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before moving to a rack."

Browning butter cooks its milk solids until they toast, which adds a caramel, hazelnut-like flavor to an otherwise classic cookie. It also cooks off some water, so these bake up chewier.

The extra egg yolk adds richness and chew. Pull the cookies while the centers still look a little underdone; they finish setting on the hot sheet.
