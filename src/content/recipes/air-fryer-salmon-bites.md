steps:
  - "Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for 3 minutes."
  - "Pat the salmon cubes dry. Toss with the oil, garlic powder, paprika and salt."
  - "Spread the salmon in the basket in a single layer with space between pieces. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, shaking once, until the edges are browned and the salmon reaches 145°F (63°C)."
  - "While it cooks, stir the honey, soy sauce, grated garlic and lemon juice together."
  - "Toss the hot salmon bites with the glaze and top with green onion. Serve over rice or salad."

Cutting salmon into cubes gives more surface for browning and cuts cooking time to under 10 minutes. The air fryer's dry heat crisps the edges while the centers stay tender.

The glaze goes on after cooking. Honey burns easily at 400°F, and tossing the hot bites in it gives a glossy coat without any scorching.
