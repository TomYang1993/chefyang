This is a weeknight take on the flavors of bulgogi, using ground beef instead of thinly sliced steak. It costs less and cooks in a fraction of the time.

Gochujang is optional but worth buying: a spoonful adds gentle heat and a fermented depth that soy sauce alone can't give.
